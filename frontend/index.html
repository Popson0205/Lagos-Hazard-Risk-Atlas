          GIS Submission Standards Update

          Raster datasets should be Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs in EPSG:4326 or EPSG:32631, with a declared unit, resolution and acquisition date. Vector datasets should be GeoJSON with a risk_class property per feature.
